🌴 HALALŪAU™

Our traditional Hawaiian halal feast.

Whole Goat Head Halalūau --- A whole slow-roasted goat head
decorated with pineapple rings, accompanied by charred goat offal,
coconut rice, pickled turnips and macaroni salad. Ceremonial paper
umbrella included.

Luau Goat Shawarma --- Spit-roasted goat shawarma with toum, pickles
and hot pineapple relish, rolled in saj and inexplicably wrapped in a
banana leaf.

Pineapple Goat Entrails --- Charred goat offal tossed with grilled
pineapple, onions, chilies and a sweet teriyaki-pomegranate glaze.

🍍 SHAWAIIAN

The ancient art of putting tropical fruit where it wasn't requested.

Shawaiian Shawarma --- Chicken shawarma, toum, pickled turnips,
fries and pineapple relish.

Shawaiian Goat Deluxe --- Pulled goat, mango amba, grilled
pineapple, cabbage and garlic sauce in pita.

Shawaiian Loaded Fries --- Fries buried beneath shawarma, cheese,
toum, pineapple salsa and sesame seeds.

🏝️ HAWAI'ALAL

"The taste of Hawaii, but halal." --- Owner

Hawai'alal Loco Moco --- Coconut rice topped with a goat-kofta
patty, fried egg, mushroom gravy and crispy onions.

Hawai'alal Musubi --- Halal luncheon meat glazed with teriyaki and
date molasses, pressed onto saffron rice and wrapped in nori.

Hawai'alal Fried Rice --- Rice, shawarma chicken, scrambled egg,
pineapple, sumac, scallions and enough soy sauce to obscure its country
of origin entirely.

🌺 ALOHALAL

Where hospitality meets dietary compliance.

Alohalal Burger --- Goat patty, halloumi, grilled pineapple, pickled
turnips and spicy toum.

Alohalal Breakfast --- Hummus, fried eggs, halal sausage, grilled
pineapple, pita and a scoop of Hawaiian macaroni salad.

Alohalal Platter for Two --- Goat kebab, chicken shawarma, falafel,
coconut rice, hummus, pineapple slaw and two completely unnecessary
orchid garnishes.

🤙 MAHALOAL

Management believes "Mahalo" means delicious.

Mahaloal Mixed Grill --- Goat chops, kofta and chicken kebab glazed
with pomegranate-teriyaki sauce.

Mahaloal Mac Salad --- Hawaiian macaroni salad with cucumber,
parsley, mint, sumac and tahini. Neither ancestor approves.

🐐 KEBABALOHA

Meat. Fire. Pineapple. Repeat.

Kebabaloha Skewer --- Alternating chunks of goat, onion and
pineapple, lacquered with date-teriyaki glaze.

Coconut Goat Kofta --- Goat kofta swimming in coconut-tahini sauce
and topped with toasted coconut.

Volcano Shish Tawook --- Spicy chicken skewers served inside half a
pineapple because apparently we own a pineapple corer now.

🌊 POLYNESIYRIAN

"A treasured cuisine of the Polynesiyrian people."

Damascus Poké --- Shawarma beef over rice with cucumber, seaweed,
pickled turnips, hummus, sesame and mango.

Tropical Kibbeh --- Fried kibbeh stuffed with goat, pine nuts and
mango, with passion-fruit tahini.

Macaroni Tabbouleh --- Macaroni salad assaulted with parsley, mint,
tomato, cucumber and lemon.

🗿 LEVANTIKI

Modern Levantine cuisine, served with tiny umbrellas whether
appropriate or not.

Tiki Toum Wings --- Chicken wings glazed with pineapple-chili sauce
and served with an unreasonable quantity of garlic sauce.

Falafel Pineapple Pizza --- Flatbread topped with falafel,
mozzarella, pineapple, tahini and pickled turnips.

Hummus Volcano --- Hummus piled around a central mound of
flaming-looking spicy braised goat, surrounded by pita "lava rocks." No
actual tableside fire; management has been spoken to.

🌯 SHAWARMALOHA

Our signature mistake.

The Original Shawarmaloha™ --- Goat shawarma, pineapple relish,
toum, pickles, fries and teriyaki sauce.

Shawarmaloha Supreme --- The above, plus halloumi, mango amba and a
fried egg.

Shawarmaloha Ohana Size --- A preposterously large shawarma intended
for four people and garnished with an entire pineapple crown.

☪️ ISLAMIC-PACIFIC FUSION

The owner's preferred term when speaking to food journalists.

Deconstructed Goat Halalūau Experience --- Braised goat, coconut
rice, tahini, pineapple reduction, pickled turnips and a single mint
leaf. Costs considerably more because it is "deconstructed."

Pacific-Rim Mansaf --- Goat, rice, yogurt sauce, macadamias and
grilled pineapple.

Imam's Surf & Turf* --- Goat chops and grilled fish with
coconut-tahini sauce, rice and pineapple salsa.

*Management has not consulted an imam regarding this dish.
